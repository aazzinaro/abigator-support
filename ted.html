Ted

A private household assistant · Privacy Policy · Effective November 4, 2026

What Ted is

Ted is a private tool used only by the Azzinaro household. It is not offered to the public, and nobody outside the family can sign in to it.

What Ted accesses

With each account owner's permission, Ted reads email and calendar events read-only to write short daily summaries for that same person. Ted cannot send, delete or change email or calendar events.

Where the data goes

Summaries are delivered only to the account owner. Mail is processed on a computer in the household and by Anthropic's Claude to write the summaries. Ted does not sell, share or advertise with any data, and keeps no copies of email beyond what a summary needs.

Removing access

Any account owner can remove Ted's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
