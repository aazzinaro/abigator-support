About the app

Word Construction Zone is a construction-site word game for young children (about ages 4 to 6). Kids build three-letter words from wooden letter blocks that say their sounds, make word families with a crane, read a word and find its picture, and smash the finished word for fun. It needs no account and works without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what does the unlock cost?

The first groups of words in Build a Word and the first word families in Word Family Crane are free, and so is a first set of Read & Find words. A single one-time purchase, Unlock Everything, opens every group and family for good, and everything added in future updates. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

How do I unlock everything?

Open a locked group and tap “For grown-ups”, answer the short grown-ups question, and the unlock page opens. You can also reach it from Settings (the gear on the start screen, behind the same question). Children never see a price or a buy button on their own.

I bought it already but the games are still locked.

Open the unlock page and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. A purchase can be shared with your family through Apple Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

The sound or voice is too loud, or I want it off.

Sound effects and the spoken voice each have their own switch and volume slider in Settings. The speaker button on the start screen mutes or unmutes both.

Does my child’s progress get lost?

Progress (which words are built and word families finished) is stored on your device only. It stays when you update the app, but deleting the app removes it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in landscape orientation.
