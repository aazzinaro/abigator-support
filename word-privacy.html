Privacy Policy

Word Construction Zone · Abigator Productions · Effective November 4, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

The short version

Word Construction Zone does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no links that leave the app. The app is designed for young children, and it is safe for them to use.

What the app stores on your device

To remember which words have been built, which word families are finished, and your settings (sound, voice, hints), the app saves that information on your device only. We never receive it, and it is removed when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we do not use the microphone or camera. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media code.

Children’s privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, the app is consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Anything that involves money is behind a grown-ups question, and children never see a price.

Purchases

The optional “Unlock Everything” purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple’s own privacy policy. We receive no payment details and no information about who you are. The app only learns, on your device, whether the purchase has been made.

Spoken words

The voice that says letter sounds and words uses the speech built into your iPhone or iPad and runs on the device. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
